Financial reports

Every figure below is computed live from the double-entry ledger — nothing is cached or hardcoded. Change a date range and the report recomputes immediately.

Profit & loss

Income and expenses grouped by account, with net profit. The core statement for asking "did the month make money?"

Balance sheet

Assets, liabilities and equity as of a date. Includes a live check that assets equal liabilities plus equity.

Cash flow

Inflows versus outflows across your cash and bank accounts for a date range.

Taxable income

Net profit for the period plus the sales tax collected as a liability. For filing, not for paying taxes.
