Open-source QuickBooks Online alternative — v{{ version }}

{{ app_name }} is a self-hosted accounting and invoicing app for freelancers and micro-businesses. It gives you real double-entry bookkeeping, multi-line invoicing, expense tracking and live financial reports — all in one FastAPI application with a single SQLite file, no cloud account and no monthly fee.

Why it exists

QuickBooks Online charges $35/month per user and more for anything beyond the basics. Odoo and ERPNext are full enterprise suites whose accounting module is powerful but heavy to install and operate — far more than a solo consultant or a two-person studio needs.

{{ app_name }} takes the opposite approach: one file, one database, zero configuration. It is deliberately small so you can read the whole thing, fork it, and make it yours.

What it does

  • Invoicing — multi-line invoices with configurable tax, draft → sent → paid lifecycle, printable HTML.
  • Expenses — category, vendor, amount, receipt note; CSV bulk import.
  • Accounting — simple double-entry journal with a seeded chart of accounts you can extend.
  • Reports — profit & loss, balance sheet, cash flow, taxable income — computed live from the ledger.
  • API — JSON REST endpoints for invoices, expenses, customers and reports.

What it is not

This is an honest scope statement, not a disclaimer: {{ app_name }} is not GAAP or IFRS audit-compliant, does not handle multi-currency translation, payroll, bank reconciliation, payables, or tax filing. If you need those, use a purpose-built accounting suite. If you are a freelancer or a small business that just needs to invoice, track spend, and see whether the month was profitable — this is for you.
